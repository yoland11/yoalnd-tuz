# Vehicle partnership: partner expenses, reimbursements and printing

The existing vehicle workspace is `/admin/vehicles/:id?tab=partnership`. This extension uses the original single partnered vehicle, original parties, expenses, monthly closing, financial approvals, MASTER cashbox, canonical payment state, and printing helpers. It adds no schema, transport record, vehicle, salary, cashbox or expense system.

## Employee workflow

1. In **النشاط والمصاريف**, choose an existing vehicle expense under **مصروف مدفوع من الشريك**. Select the actual payer from the agreement effective on the expense date, and explicitly choose an operating cost or capital investment and a reimbursable advance or capital contribution. No amount is re-entered.
2. Submit it to the existing financial approvals. An outstanding original company cash request must first be cancelled through its original workflow. An expense already paid by the company cannot also become a partner expense.
3. Approval creates the original noncash expense/investment and corresponding advance or capital right once. Capital contributions do not become reimbursable advances or change ownership percentages.
4. **المستحقات والتسويات** offers **طلب تسديد سلفة** against an approved original advance, including before monthly closing and after economic partnership ownership ends. The original dated payee stays unchanged. Partial requests reserve only their own amount. Administration must approve the original pending financial request before cash and paid balances change.
5. **الكشوف والطباعة** provides current partner/vehicle statements in A4/PDF, frozen approved-month reports, and 80mm receipts for actually executed profit, advance and driver payouts. Receipt numbering reuses the original FIN number. Reprinting never creates a transaction.
6. Administration can select an approved month in **الكشوف والطباعة** and choose **ربط دفعات السائقين بالشهر**. Confirm the reason to classify that month's already executed driver payments inside its available approved internal conversions. The result shows each original FIN, its paid amount, covered amount and amount already paid outside the conversion. This is a classification of existing cash, including payments made before closing, and never another amount due or another cash payment. Existing nonzero mappings remain immutable; repeating the operation reuses them.
7. To return an executed profit distribution, partner advance repayment or driver payment, administration opens its **original FIN** in **الصندوق الرئيسي** and uses the existing **عكس** action with a reason. This is a full return of that original payment only. It creates one cash-in and opposite liability journal, retains the original FIN and allocations, and restores the canonical outstanding balance. An exact retry uses the same saved return. A previously distributed profit month becomes approved again if its derived balance is unpaid. A new payment still requires the original approval workflow.
8. **سجل الصرف المعكوس** in **الكشوف والطباعة** links back to each original FIN and its return history. A reversed payment cannot be printed as a current executed payout receipt. Driver coverage explicitly identifies fully returned payments and excludes them from new coverage, while an independently approved replacement payment remains eligible.

## Financial caller impact

- `routes.ts` exposes only source-owned cost and advance-request commands on the original scoped vehicle endpoint, with the original financial writer injected.
- `accounting.ts` dispatches the trusted advance reference; it rejects mixed distribution/advance payloads. All existing activity, cost, capital, monthly and driver producers retain their own paths.
- `master-cash-box.ts` adds an advance source preflight/finalizer to the existing principal-only cash block. It posts `VP-ADVANCE / 1000`, not another expense, and keeps operating revenue/expense totals unchanged.
- `posting.ts` directs rejection/cancellation of original advance transactions to their source proof and canonical reconciliation. Noncash reversal keeps its original handler. The three executed cash payout types use the existing MASTER reversal transaction with source-owned admission and finalization.
- `workspace.ts` adds a bounded read-only association with the original expense's approved month for the advance button. The server independently re-proves the frozen cost, agreement, advance and financial origin before any request or approval.
- No existing booking prices, invoices, protected A4 designs, payroll amounts, inventory movements or historical financial rows are rewritten.

### Driver conversion classification

The source-owned `closings/driver-coverage` POST is admin-only and has no financial writer. `driver-coverage.ts` validates the selected version/digest, the original approved month and its journal, frozen wage sources and existing conversion parents, then re-proves each original executed driver payment. It appends coverage allocations and separate immutable mapping/operation audits in one transaction. Original driver request proposals, settlement IDs and cash/ledger rows remain unchanged. One stable nonce replays its saved result before mutable month checks; a new attempt reuses any existing nonzero payment mapping. Queries are bounded and a mismatch fails the complete transaction.

The shared right canonical adapter accepts only this exact proven executed driver source with conversion coverage. `driver-cash-source.ts` independently verifies the original settlement, wage, funding, expense, approved execution metadata, cash balance difference and original accrual/cash journals. `driver-coverage-proof.ts` validates immutable mapping hashes, original financial request proofs and the frozen wage/revision and conversion parents. These read-only helpers do not call full driver payout preflight, avoiding recursive reconciliation. The original employee canonical path still counts the driver's actual cash once. The blast radius is the existing right reconciliation dispatcher (workspace, statements, payout capacity and monthly unused/reopen guards); every other source retains its original contract.

### Full executed cash return

`cash-reversal.ts` reuses the original profit/advance/driver execution admission on the first return, then validates the original request audit, executed FIN metadata, exact cash difference, allocations and journals. Source/month/booking locks precede the existing MASTER lock. MASTER posts the full original amount once using exact cents, a typed cash-in reversal, and `1000 / VP-PROFIT`, `1000 / VP-ADVANCE` or `1000 / VP-DRIVER`. Operating income/expenses and profit remain unchanged; this is not new revenue or another cost. All links, audits, canonical reconciliation and derived monthly status changes share the same transaction, including existing callbacks.

`cash-reversal-proof.ts` is an independent read-only history proof. It validates the immutable source, mapping, exact original/reverse identities and both actual journals without importing mutable source admission or payment reconciliation. Exact replay therefore remains valid after replacement payments or a later valid monthly reopening. For mapped drivers, the immutable proof is appended inside the transaction before canonical reconciliation needs to read it; any later failure rolls back proof, cash and journal together. Fully returned historical driver cash uses this proof rather than re-admitting a cancelled old wage. Missing/tampered/partial proof fails explicitly and is never hidden as an empty payment list.

Driver coverage summary adds the optional `reversedPayments` count only when a caller opts in with `includeReversalSummary: true`. Legacy request bodies, response shape and durable retry fingerprints remain unchanged. The current UI opts in and displays the exclusion count.

## Cash and concurrency safeguards

The transaction acquires partnership root, original booking/month source locks, vehicle and cost locks, rights and original finance proofs, and only then the original MASTER posting boundary. The root serializes partnership requests. Durable nonce replay precedes mutable source/capacity reads. Immutable request audits and exact primary/coverage allocations are validated before approval; only this request's proven pending holds are added back.

The original approved cost must occur exactly once in the frozen month, with matching funding ID, original expense, original noncash financial ID, amount, date, source snapshot and digest. A zero month additionally uses the existing approved-month reader to prove the original administrative zero-approval audit. Cash is mapped inside real approved internal conversions through the same payout FI, never by creating another gross payout.

## Verification and explicit limits

Focused tests exercise exact large money, original dated payers, source mismatch, approval, partial cash payments, retry, overpayment, duplicate/forged allocations, rejected/cancelled holds, audit rollback, zero-month approval proof, capital separation, frozen reports and receipt source/ledger proof. Driver classification tests also cover payments predating monthly approval, missing execution metadata, immutable wage-month proof, same-FI identity, partial/no conversion capacity, original driver balances, changed targets, admin-only access and controlled atomic rollback. Full-return tests cover all three cash sources, retry after month changes, mapped-driver proof ordering, exact large cash-in, unchanged operating totals, original settlements, source/audit/canonical/callback failures, replacement driver cash, legacy API compatibility, safe error contracts and duplicate-notification prevention. The actual original MASTER cash function is exercised under a controlled transaction adapter. Typecheck, build, payment-state and critical gates must pass before release.

Live database write/concurrency/save-smoke coverage is **SKIPPED** when there is no separately verified TEST database; controlled tests do not claim PostgreSQL concurrency proof. No Production business diagnostic writes are permitted. No new migration is needed; previously activated migration 0129 must not be reapplied.

New UI advance requests use an opt-in source-only v2 proposal; saved v1 proposals retain their original money, command and fingerprint. Historical v1 monthly settlements may use only already-approved frozen periods after ownership ends. Cash return currently supports the full original payment only; partial cash returns are not supported. A first return must still prove the original eligible source; historical sources with missing proof require review rather than inferred values. Driver conversion classification requires the original approved wage month; it does not invent missing historical sources, reassign cash to another month, or silently top up an existing immutable partial mapping. A physical thermal printer/PDF device test is not implied by HTML/layout tests or opening a browser print dialog.

## Historical settlements and opening loss

1. Administration opens **تسويات الفترات** on the original vehicle partnership page. **إثبات الخسارة الافتتاحية** reads the amount and original loss shares from the first approved agreement. Supply an evidence reference and reason; submit the existing central pending financial request and approve its exact FIN through the original MASTER approval screen. This posts one noncash VP-LOSS / VP-OPENING-CLEAR journal, never operating income, an expense payment or a cash movement. No arbitrary amount override is accepted.
2. Approve the first actual monthly closing normally. It materializes original-party opening loss rights once, even with zero monthly activity. Income can recover those same rights in that month or later. A controlled unused-month reopening reverses derived revision rights, retaining the original opening source. Used/pending opening evidence cannot be reversed; approved older months are not recalculated from current percentages.
3. Under **تسويات الفترات السابقة**, select a later open month and review the existing executed, unconsumed FIN. Confirm its original amount/date and the evidence reference/reason. Only the report classification date changes. Already used or pending-frozen sources cannot move; unused classifications can be revoked by another immutable audit. Monetary corrections must first exist in their original authorized source; the tool cannot invent historical profit or cash.
4. In **الكشوف والطباعة**, select an approved month and use **ربط دفعات السلف بالشهر**. Existing executed source-only advance repayments are classified inside that month's real internal conversion capacity using the SAME original FIN and primary allocation. Original proposals stay frozen. The result separately reports covered and already-paid-outside-conversion amounts; an uncovered amount is never another amount due. A genuine zero-capacity month creates no fake mapping or payment. Repeated commands replay exact saved results; existing nonzero mappings are immutable.
5. Full cash returns keep the original source and mapping. After a verified return, replacement repayment still requires a new pending request and approval. Fully returned cash is excluded from later coverage only after immutable original/reverse FIN, audit and journal proof. No old request, loss agreement or cash transaction is silently overwritten.

The new routes are narrow additions under the original vehicle endpoint: `opening-loss`, `period-attributions` (and its unused-only `revoke`), and `closings/advance-coverage`. They preserve AJN structured failures and server permissions. Historical approved-obligation locking is a server-only opt-in; it cannot enable activity, draft-period approval or a client financial override. No new database migration is required. Production diagnosis and visual QA perform no business writes.

## Current advance receipts and zero-journal period recovery

- Executed source-only advance repayments (proposal v2) use the same existing 80mm preview and receipt as legacy repayments. Select the original FIN under **الكشوف والطباعة**. Its original approval, actual cash deduction, VP-ADVANCE journal, source digest and original partner identity must match. A pending or reversed payout cannot become a current receipt. Monthly closing or current ownership is not a prerequisite for printing an already executed original repayment. Later conversion mapping leaves the original receipt amount and FIN unchanged.
- Under **الإغلاق الشهري → استعادة الإغلاقات الخالية من القيود**, choose **إلغاء الإغلاق المعلق** or, for administration, **إعادة فتح الإغلاق**. Review the original month/revision, enter a reason and explicitly confirm returning it to draft. The original server checks the exact saved version, zero journal, approval evidence and unused rights. An accounting result of zero alone never authorizes this operation.
- A lost response retains **متابعة المحاولة الأصلية → إعادة المحاولة بنفس المرجع**, including after switching months or refreshing source data. This reuses the same original command. A saved operation and a subsequent display-refresh error are reported separately.
- A period with an original financial FIN does not use zero-journal recovery. Its original MASTER reversal workflow still needs a separate HTTP/UI integration correction to forward the required closing version and digest; this change does not bypass that barrier.
